Student Privacy First

Privacy Policy

Your academic progress, notes, and study hours belong strictly to you. Learn how StudyAI collects, protects, and isolates your student information.

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 2.4
The StudyAI Privacy Promise

We will never sell your personal information, train public third-party commercial AI models on your private study notes, or display invasive targeted advertising trackers. StudyAI is built for students, not data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide intelligent academic recommendations and track habits, we collect only the minimal necessary data:

  • Identity & Authentication Data: Your name, college/personal email address, and an encrypted hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Academic Management Data: Course names (e.g., "Machine Learning"), color tags, task descriptions, deadlines, estimated hours, and completion timestamps.
  • Study Habit Logs: Logged daily study hours, session dates, and voluntary reflection notes used to calculate consistency streaks.
  • Profile Assets: Selected avatar preset or uploaded profile photos (processed securely via Cloudinary).

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your academic information strictly to deliver and improve core platform functionalities:

  • Heuristic Ranking: Calculating the Urgency & Deficit Index (UDI) to recommend which subjects need immediate study focus.
  • Automated Pomodoro Timetables: Slicing your chosen daily study target into 50-minute focus blocks with 10-minute breaks.
  • Progress & Habit Streaks: Computing your continuous active study streak counter (🔥 Days) and rendering interactive Chart.js graphs.
  • Account Maintenance: Enabling secure login sessions and password recovery.

3. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We implement rigorous defense-in-depth technical safeguards to protect your records:

Werkzeug Hash Protection
Passwords are run through industry-grade PBKDF2/SHA256 salted hashing before ever touching the database.
Database Isolation
Each student's records are bound to their private foreign user ID with cascading relational safety.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

StudyAI uses essential browser mechanisms strictly for functionality:

  • Session Cookie (Flask-Login): Maintains your authenticated session so you don't need to log in on every page reload.
  • Browser LocalStorage: Stores your study_theme preference (e.g. "light" or "dark") locally on your device.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising pixels, Google Analytics trackers, or cross-site tracking scripts.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

StudyAI utilizes select high-reliability infrastructure partners to host our services:

  • Cloudinary: Used securely to optimize and host optional custom student avatar pictures over HTTPS.
  • Turso (libSQL): Encrypted distributed cloud database infrastructure for production persistence.
  • Render: Cloud application hosting with TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit.

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure

You have complete control over your academic data. At any time, you can:

  • Update or edit your subjects, deadlines, target study hours, and profile details via the Settings page.
  • Delete individual tasks or entire subjects (which automatically triggers cascading deletion of associated records).
  • Request full erasure of your account by contacting support@studyai.internal.
