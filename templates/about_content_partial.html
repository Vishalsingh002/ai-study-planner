The Genesis

The Story Behind StudyAI

Like thousands of college students, creator Vishu Singh encountered the universal academic paradox: modern students possess access to limitless learning resources, yet struggle intensely with executive function and task prioritization.

Traditional calendar apps simply log static events, while to-do lists treat a high-stakes Machine Learning semester exam the same as a casual 10-minute reading assignment. The result is chronic procrastination, last-night all-nighters, and high academic anxiety.

StudyAI was built to serve as an empathetic, intelligent academic co-pilot that continually calculates what you should study right now based on cold, objective mathematical urgency.

The Innovation

The Urgency & Deficit Index (UDI)

Unlike black-box generative models that can produce inconsistent or ungrounded schedules, StudyAI implements a deterministic Multi-Criteria Urgency Model:

UDI = (Syllabus Deficit × 45) + (Average Task Urgency × 0.55) + Overdue Booster
• Syllabus Deficit: (1.0 - Completed Tasks / Total Tasks) × 45
• Task Urgency: Non-linear decay from 100 (overdue) down to 20 (> 7 days)
• Priority Factor: High (+30), Medium (+15), Low (+0)

This formulation guarantees that neglected courses with looming deadlines are prioritized, while subjects with 90%+ completion rates are preserved without monopolizing your daily study energy.

The 50/10 Pomodoro Rhythm

Cognitive neuroscience proves that continuous attention decays significantly after 45-50 minutes. StudyAI automates:

  • 50 Min Focus Block: Monotask on single highest-priority topic.
  • 10 Min Recovery Buffer: Physical movement and brain recharge.
  • Daily Goal Cap: Prevents counter-productive sleep deprivation.
